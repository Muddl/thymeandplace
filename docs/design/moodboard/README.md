## Why five are missing

The six images were attached to the task, but the files listed as saved for the agent did not exist on its disk, so their bytes could not be copied. Only #1 was re-fetched, from a public-domain source, because it is the same cover.

- **#2 and #3** are public-domain scans too. A search of the Internet Archive and Commons did not turn up the exact editions (the 1898 Allen guide, and the Perry cover with the basket, corn and spade). Perry catalogues for 1892, 1894, 1895 and 1898 exist in the same USDA collection, but their covers differ.
- **#4 to #6** are photographs, not public domain. Two carry a stock-agency watermark. They should only be committed if Wade holds a licence for them, or he may prefer to keep them out of the repo.

To finish this folder, drop the original files here with the same numbered names.

## What the design variants took from the board

- From the covers: the 1899 cover's fat-face display type and its vermilion and ultramarine on white lettering (variant A); the catalogue habit of numbered entries and double rules (A); the chromolithograph tomato, cut from a different public-domain cover (see `docs/design/README.md`).
- From the stalls: black framing, white sign boards with crisp capitals, the ordered green crate (variant B), and the plain white ground and grid that all three variants share.
